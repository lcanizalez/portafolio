About Me

I partner with teams to identify repetitive, error-prone processes and convert them into automated, scalable solutions. Using modern web technologies and Google Workspace automation, I build tools that standardize data, enforce validation, and integrate systems—so sales, marketing, and operations teams can work faster and with greater confidence. My approach balances rapid delivery with maintainability: I prototype quickly, validate with users, then harden solutions for production. The result is measurable: fewer manual steps, fewer data errors, faster cycle times, and clearer operational visibility. I bring a pragmatic mindset to digital transformation—prioritizing impact, usability, and continuous improvement.
